FAQ

What is the visiting charge?

The mandatory visiting charge is ₹49. For a night job booked after 8:00 PM, it is ₹99.

Are repair and material costs included in the visit charge?

No. The visit charge covers the technician visit. Any repair labour or material cost is explained after inspection and before work begins.

Do you cover all of Dehradun?

We serve Dehradun. Share your area when booking and we will confirm availability.

How do I book?

You can call us, WhatsApp us, or use the request form above. We will confirm your booking before sending a technician.
